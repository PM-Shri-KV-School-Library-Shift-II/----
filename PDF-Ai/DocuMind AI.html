Saved Notes

Your personal library — save, search, and organize all your generated content.

  • Save — Click the bookmark icon on any generated output (summary, notes, quiz, etc.)
  • Search — Find notes instantly by title, content, or tags
  • Pin/Unpin — Keep important notes at the top
  • View — Click any saved note to open it with full markdown & math rendering
  • Export — Download all notes as JSON backup — import anytime to restore
  • Keyboard shortcut — Press Ctrl+K to toggle the panel

Privacy & Settings

Your data stays on your device. Full control over your experience.

  • API Key — Stored only in your browser's localStorage. Never sent anywhere except OpenRouter
  • Notes — Saved in IndexedDB (browser database). Export as JSON anytime
  • Model Selection — Choose from 8+ free AI models or enter any OpenRouter model ID
  • Replay Guide — Click "Show Welcome Again" in Settings to replay this guide
  • No accounts — No sign-up, no passwords, no cloud. Just you and your browser
